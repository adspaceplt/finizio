Services

What we design, build and install

Everything is custom-made to your measurements. From design and manufacture through to assembly, renovation works and installation, the job stays with our own team and our own plant.

Kitchen cabinets

Top and bottom units, tall and pantry units, island counters, and full wet–dry layouts planned around your appliances. We check socket and plumbing positions at measurement stage so nothing has to be re-cut later.

Wardrobes

Sliding, swing-door and open-concept walk-ins, fitted floor-to-ceiling so you don't lose the dead space above the frame. Internal layouts planned around what you actually hang and fold.

TV consoles & feature walls

Living-hall consoles, feature panelling and display shelving, matched to the same finish family as your kitchen so the home reads as one scheme.

Renovation works

Hacking, tiling, plumbing and electrical handled alongside the carpentry, so one team is accountable for the whole job rather than you coordinating separate trades around each other.

Vanity, study & storage

Bathroom vanity units, study desks, shoe cabinets and store-room shelving. Usually quoted alongside a kitchen or wardrobe package.

Our material

HMR melamine particleboard

Every cabinet we make is built from high moisture-resistant (HMR) melamine particleboard, chosen for how it holds up in a working Malaysian kitchen.

Water resistance

Resists moisture absorption, making it ideal for kitchens.

Low formaldehyde

Low formaldehyde emissions, a safer choice for indoor use.

Low maintenance

Easy to clean, and does not need sealing or polishing.

Strong & durable

Hard-wearing, resisting scratches, stains and heat.

Cost effective

More affordable than solid wood or plywood, without giving up the qualities above.

How it works

Our process

Tell us about the space

Message us on WhatsApp with your floor plan or a few photos, and we'll reply the same day with an indicative budget.

Site measurement

We measure on site, check plumbing and power points, and talk through the layout: wet and dry separation, appliance sizes, socket positions.

3D design & itemized quote

A 3D visual of your space plus a written quotation listing every material and fitting, revised until the layout is right.

Manufacturing

Your units are made in our own plant. Countertop templating happens once the base units are confirmed on site.

Installation

Our team installs, aligns and finishes on site, together with any renovation works in your scope.

Handover

A final walkthrough of the finished job, with care instructions for your new kitchen.
