Why Finizio

You should know what you're paying for

Our own manufacturing plant

We don't subcontract the build. Cutting, edging and assembly happen under our own roof, where our craftsmen check the work before it ever reaches your home.

We name the material, in writing

Your quotation states the carcass board, door finish, countertop and hardware brand — line by line. If a quote elsewhere just says "kitchen cabinet, RM 20,000", you have no idea what's inside it.

Moisture-resistant construction

Malaysian kitchens are humid and wet work is constant. We build carcasses to resist moisture rather than using particle board, which swells and crumbles once water reaches the core.

An itemized quotation

Your quote breaks materials and fittings down line by line, so you can compare it fairly against anyone else's and know exactly what you are signing off on.

How it works

From first message to handover

Tell us about the space

Message us on WhatsApp with your floor plan or a few photos. We'll give you a rough budget band straight away, before anyone visits.

Site measurement

We measure on site, check plumbing and power points, and discuss layout — wet and dry separation, appliance sizes, socket positions.

3D design & itemized quote

You get a 3D visual plus a written quotation listing every material and fitting. Revisions until the layout is right.

Manufacturing

Your units are fabricated in our own plant. Countertop templating is done after the base units are confirmed on site.

Installation & handover

Our team installs, aligns and finishes on site, then walks through the completed job with you along with care instructions.

Common questions

Before you ask

How much does a kitchen cabinet cost?

Cabinetry is priced per foot run, so the total depends on your wall lengths plus the door finish, carcass board and countertop you choose. Send your floor plan over on WhatsApp and we will come back with an indicative budget — a firm figure needs an on-site measurement.

Which areas do you cover?

We work throughout Negeri Sembilan — Seremban, Nilai, Sendayan and Port Dickson — and regularly take on projects in Kajang, Semenyih, Bangi and the surrounding southern Klang Valley. Message us with your location and we'll confirm.

How long does the whole process take?

It depends on the size of the job and the materials you choose — we confirm a schedule with you at quotation stage, before any deposit. Countertop fabrication adds time after the base units are installed.

Do you handle hacking, tiling and plumbing?

Yes. We take on the renovation as a whole — hacking, tiling, plumbing and electrical alongside the carpentry — so you deal with one team instead of coordinating several trades yourself. Tell us the full scope and we'll quote it as one package.
