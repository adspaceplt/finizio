Understanding your quote

What moves the price

Why is cabinetry priced per foot?

Cabinets are built in runs along a wall, so cost scales with length rather than area. Measure the wall lengths your cabinets will occupy, multiply by the rate for top and bottom separately, then add the countertop.

Door finish

The single biggest lever. Melamine is the value option; aluminium and 4G resist heat and moisture better; acrylic gives the highest gloss at the highest price.

Countertop material

Quartz and granite cost more than compact laminate but resist stains and heat far better. On a typical kitchen the countertop is a meaningful share of the total.

Layout complexity

L-shape and straight runs are the most economical. Islands, curved ends, tall units and appliance housings each add fabrication time.
