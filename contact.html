Before you visit

What to bring

You'll get far more out of the first conversation with these to hand.

Your floor plan

The developer's layout drawing, or a photo of it. Even a rough sketch with wall lengths helps.

Photos of the space

Wide shots of the kitchen or bedroom as it stands today, including where the pipes and sockets are.

Appliance list

Hob, hood, oven, fridge and washing machine dimensions — these determine the cabinet openings.

A budget range

Even a rough band. It lets us steer you to the right materials instead of quoting something unrealistic.

Book a site measurement

We'll measure on site and put the quotation in writing, itemized line by line.
